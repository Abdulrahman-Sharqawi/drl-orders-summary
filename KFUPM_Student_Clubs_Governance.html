DRL Clubs Platform

DRL-hosted infrastructure  ·  Student-owned execution  ·  Engineer guidance on demand

Open Platform Example

The DRL Clubs Platform is the single digital home for every Engineering Excellence Club at KFUPM — from registration and attendance to public showcases and calendar visibility. It is hosted by DRL, student-executed, and open to the whole university community.

Why This Platform Exists
Six real problems. Twelve platform ideas. One integrated solution.
Every feature below was designed to fix a specific failure mode in how KFUPM clubs operate today — not as a nice-to-have tool, but as a direct response to a problem leaders, members, sponsors, and DRL face every semester.
Problem 01
The club dies when members graduate.
Every year a new team starts from scratch because nothing was documented or preserved — institutional knowledge walks out the door with every graduating class.
Platform ideas that fix it
Knowledge Wiki Member Portfolio Club Micro-Website
Problem 02
Nobody knows what anyone is actually doing.
Leaders can't manage what they can't see, and DRL can't support clubs they have no data on — activity, attendance, and spend all happen off the record.
Platform ideas that fix it
KPI Dashboard Attendance & QR Tracking Budgeting & Invoicing
Problem 03
Good work goes unrecognized.
Students do real engineering work that never shows up on a resume or gets acknowledged by anyone — effort disappears into the semester with no lasting record.
Platform ideas that fix it
Rewards & LPI Member Portfolio
Problem 04
Finding and joining a club is chaotic.
Students don't know what clubs exist, what roles are open, or when anything is happening — discovery is word-of-mouth and onboarding is inconsistent.
Platform ideas that fix it
Club Directory Registration & Onboarding KFUPM Calendar Integration
Problem 05
Sponsors get nothing after writing a check.
Companies get no visibility into how their money is being used or what impact it had — which is exactly why sponsorships don't renew.
Platform ideas that fix it
Sponsor Visibility Portal
Problem 06
Consultants and mentors are disconnected.
Expert oversight is informal and invisible — check-ins happen but nothing is logged, risks get flagged verbally and then forgotten.
Platform ideas that fix it
Consultant & Mentor Hub
12 Platform Features — Mapped to the Problems Above
📅
KFUPM Calendar Integration
Fixes: Chaotic discovery
All club events, workshops, competitions, and social activities are published directly to the official KFUPM campus calendar — visible to every student, faculty member, and department.
Live sync with KFUPM calendar Filter by club / domain Reminders & RSVP
📋
Student Registration & Onboarding
Fixes: Chaotic discovery
Students browse all clubs, view open roles with goals and requirements, and apply in one click. Applications are reviewed by the Training Director and tracked on the platform through acceptance, onboarding, and division placement.
Browse all clubs Open roles & goals listed Application pipeline
📍
Attendance & QR Tracking
Fixes: Zero visibility
Every session, workshop, and event generates a unique QR code. Members scan to check in — the platform records timestamp, duration, and event type. Leaders see live attendance dashboards. DRL sees club-level health at a glance.
Per-event QR codes Live leader dashboard Streak & history log
🗂️
Club Directory & Open Roles
Fixes: Chaotic discovery
A live, searchable directory of every registered club at KFUPM — including domain, current members, active projects, and open leadership roles. Each role displays its goals, required skills, and time commitment so students can make informed decisions before applying.
All KFUPM clubs Role goals & requirements Filter by domain
🌐
Club Micro-Website
Fixes: Institutional amnesia
Every club gets a dedicated micro-website hosted on the platform. It shows the current team roster, active projects with progress updates, the club's full history at KFUPM (past teams, competition results, achievements), and a public-facing portfolio of work — visible to sponsors, professors, and prospective members.
Semester team roster Project progress & history Sponsor-facing portfolio
📊
KPI Monitoring Dashboard
Fixes: Zero visibility
Real-time KPI dashboards for members, leaders, and DRL staff — attendance rates, task completion, LPI scores, machine hours, competition readiness, and design review submissions. Club leaders own their data; DRL uses aggregate views to allocate resources and support.
Member & leader views DRL aggregate view Semester reports
💰
Budgeting & Invoicing
Fixes: Zero visibility
Full budget lifecycle on the platform — from itemized request to DRL approval, purchase order generation, receipt upload, and auto-updated budget tracker. Sponsor funds are ring-fenced per club. Semester financial reports are auto-generated for DRL archives and audits.
Request → PO → receipt Sponsor ring-fencing Auto financial reports
⭐
Rewards, Badges & LPI
Fixes: Unrecognized work
The platform issues digital badges for streaks, LPI milestone certificates (LPI-1/2/3), and annual excellence awards. Machine access upgrades are triggered automatically by performance. Sponsor-backed prizes and internship fast-tracks are managed through the rewards module.
Digital badges & certs Auto machine upgrades Internship fast-track
🗃️
Member Engineering Portfolio
Fixes: Institutional amnesiaFixes: Unrecognized work
Every member gets a persistent, sharable engineering portfolio page — automatically populated from their contributions: files committed, designs uploaded, reports submitted, events attended, badges earned, and LPI score. A ready-made professional record for internship and job applications.
Auto-built from activity Shareable public link LPI score badge
🧭
Consultant & Mentor Hub
Fixes: Mentor disconnect
Every project has an assigned DRL engineer or professor consultant visible on the platform. The hub shows their availability, check-in schedule, and logged milestone sign-offs. Mentors can leave notes, flag risks, and co-sign design reviews — all tracked and visible to DRL leadership.
Consultant per project Milestone sign-off log Risk flags & notes
🤝
Sponsor Visibility Portal
Fixes: Sponsor silence
Sponsors get a dedicated login to view the clubs they support — project progress, team photos, event recaps, competition results, and how their funds were spent. Automatic quarterly impact reports are sent to each sponsor, keeping relationships warm and renewals easy.
Sponsor login & dashboard Auto quarterly reports Fund transparency
📚
Knowledge Wiki & Resource Library
Fixes: Institutional amnesia
A shared, club-contributed wiki where members document builds, publish how-to guides, upload CAD templates, and archive competition lessons learned. Resources are tagged by domain and skill level — making institutional knowledge permanent and transferable even as teams change every year.
Cross-club wiki CAD & template library Lessons learned archive
💡 Additional Platform Ideas
Inter-Club Collaboration Board — clubs post open sub-tasks for members from other clubs to contribute to, breaking silos and growing cross-domain skills.
Alumni Network Tab — past members stay connected, mentor current students, and post job or internship leads specific to KFUPM club alumni.
Club Health Leaderboard — a friendly public ranking of clubs by attendance, initiative activity, and KPI score — motivating healthy competition between clubs each semester.
DRL Machine Booking — members book DRL lab time directly through the platform, synced with the calendar and linked to their project and LPI record.
Every member's contributions are scored, visible, and cumulative. The LPI Score is your public engineering reputation at KFUPM.
📍
Attendance Score
Sessions attended ÷ sessions held. Minimum 70% to maintain Active status. Streak bonuses at 3, 6, 12 months.
Weight: 20%
✅
Task Completion Rate
Assigned tasks delivered on time vs. missed. Tracked per sprint cycle on the platform Gantt.
Weight: 25%
🗂️
Portfolio Activity
Files committed, reports submitted, designs uploaded. Each contribution timestamped and credited to the member's profile.
Weight: 20%
🔧
DRL Machine Hours
Actual vs. booked fabrication time. Efficiency score unlocks higher-tier machine access over time.
Weight: 10%
🚀
Initiative Points
Earned by running workshops, organizing events, launching side projects, or submitting open-source work. Resets each semester.
Weight: 15%
🤝
Peer Review Score
Submitted peer reviews and received ratings from teammates. Encourages collaborative accountability across the team.
Weight: 10%
LPI = (Attendance × 0.20) + (Tasks × 0.25) + (Portfolio × 0.20) + (DRL Hours × 0.10) + (Initiatives × 0.15) + (Peer Score × 0.10)  ·  Issued as LPI-1 (≥60), LPI-2 (≥75), LPI-3 (≥90)
Every leader role has a defined KPI set reviewed quarterly by the DRL team. Underperforming roles trigger a structured improvement plan.
